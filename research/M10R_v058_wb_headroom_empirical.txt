LIMITATIONS
- WB is applied to a post-demosaic identity-WB camera-RGB proxy; this does not prove Bayer-domain arithmetic placement.
- per-scene quadratic downstream fit absorbs unknown fixed/dynamic CC and tone behavior on midtones.
- the discriminator is intentionally driven by highlight information lost under one ceiling but retained under the other.
- 8-bit JPEG output may be insufficient to distinguish truncation from nearest rounding; unresolved is an acceptable result.
- no renderer assets or frozen color/tone behavior are changed by this experiment.
