M10-R 12 MP WB input review 4B — source-linked scalars and target calibration payload
2026-10-19

Result

The existing recovered IMG code exposes a useful additional observation in the
two retained original-camera JPEGs: private tag 17 is the same F+0x98 halfword
that the WB packet builder copies to P+0x36. The firmware diagnostic names that
source ExtBv. Tag 6 similarly exposes F+0x96, named IR, which the packet builder
passes through 0x420A8180 before storing P+0x34.

The current target ColorMatrix coefficients also have a concrete local firmware
source: both nine-word Q12 matrices in corrected firmware section 73,
/data/calib/B2Y_CC0_CA9_CM.bin. Every current decimal literal reproduces the
corresponding firmware coefficient rounded to eight decimal places. This is
stronger provenance than the renderer's RENDER_PARITY1A comment alone.

These results do not establish Android conversions for IR/ExtBv, physical
statistics scale, per-camera live profile state, or an illuminant assignment for
the separately recovered AWB calibration banks. They do not promote the shadow
selector to photographic WB authority.

1. New source linkage

In the original constructor at 0x421AA220, r6 is J and the established frame
structure is F = J+0x280. Descriptors at u32(M+0x23F6C) have 12-byte stride and
fields tag:u16, type:u16, count:u32, value-pointer:u32.

Tag 6:
  0x421AA4EE..0x421AA532 builds tag=6, type=8 (SSHORT), count=1,
  source=F+0x96. The descriptor index r4 is 5 after constructing tags 1..5;
  its left shift by 7 at 0x421AA51E therefore supplies J+0x280.

Tag 17:
  0x421AA534..0x421AA578 builds tag=17, type=8 (SSHORT), count=1,
  source=J+(5<<7)+0x98 = F+0x98.

Packet builder:
  0x420A84FE..0x420A8508 reads signed16 F+0x96, calls 0x420A8180,
  stores the result's halfword at P+0x34.
  0x420A850A..0x420A850E copies F+0x98 directly to P+0x36.
  0x420A8578..0x420A8592 supplies the same F+0x98 and F+0x96 fields
  to the ExtBv and IR diagnostic format slots, respectively.

The complete converter 0x420A8180..0x420A8208 returns x unchanged if x<0.
Otherwise it returns signed16 of truncation-toward-zero division by 10:
  0 <= x < 260:  (x-160)*46 / 10
  260 <= x < 360: x*42 / 10
  360 <= x < 600: x*34 / 10
  600 <= x < 1000: x*30 / 10
  1000 <= x:      x*21 / 10
No physical interpretation of these piecewise regions is assigned here.

The words in the private plaintext are directly observed. The P+0x34 conversion
is a new offline derivation using the analyzed firmware contract. The JPEGs are
from firmware 10.20.27.20; the analyzed IMG is from 30.22.23.34. They are not
captured live WB packets, and equivalence of all control-production semantics
across those versions is not established. The two-byte padding after an inline
SSHORT is ignored, rather than interpreting all four bytes as its value.

The existing 3R consumers already establish why these fields matter: signed
P+0x36 supplies the eight 0x40009E28 prior curves and signed16(P36-P34) supplies
0x400093A8's controls. The current 3Y choice P34=P36=256 deliberately leaves
the latter difference zero. The two observed source-field fixtures are therefore
useful for a later controlled offline comparison, not replacement phone values.

The payload was extracted locally from retained decoded firmware. Its MD5

corrected payload without its final four bytes.

At corrected record offsets 0x00 and 0x2C are nine signed32 coefficient words:

  Current M10_CM_D65:
    1998,-527,-199,-2392,5612,904,-739,1380,2151

Both records have scale-exponent word 12 at record+0x24, meaning denominator
4096 under the retained original v1.45 builder contract. Rounding each exact
Q12 ratio to eight decimal places, halfway away from zero, reproduces all 18
current renderer literals and all 18 golden profile literals. The maximum
absolute decimal-literal differences are 3.75e-9 and 5e-9, respectively. They
are not bit-exact double representations of the exact Q12 fractions.

The following word at record+0x28 is 2850 for the first record and 6807 for
the second. The current renderer uses reference temperatures 2856 and 6504,
and the golden fixture calls its temperature choice an accepted 17/21 reference
fixture bridge. This pass records the raw file words without newly proving
their consumer semantics or changing the current reference-temperature policy.

The retained original v1.45 contract identifies loader 0x420A4628, builder
0x420A4AAC, exact file length0x84 and config destination0x43705830. That
baseline config path is distinct from per-frame selected-gain/ASN submission.
Only the retained disassembly and contracts were used; no additional disassembler
dependency was installed and no producer or ColorSpec emulation was performed.

The independently recovered AWB banks remain B/G0/G1/R endpoint records.
The reviewed 3X basis report and 3Y adapter policy do not identify their bank
labels with the two CM endpoints. A/B storage names do not assign A/D65
illuminants. A packaged default CM source also does not prove the resident
calibration state for every camera body.

The next smallest source task is to trace record+0x28 through the original CM
initializer into its dual-matrix interpolation state, and identify the actual
resident profile and AWB-bank selection paths. Keep the existing 17/21 reference
bridge separate while answering that question. The broader numeric seam is
which verified transformation relates the target CM camera basis to the
sensor/statistics basis of the resident AWB banks, with any camera calibration
or analog-balance factors explicitly represented.

4. Negative D65 case and practical 12 MP action

The 4A RAW input verification remains valid. DNG replay exactly reproduced the
sidecar's 352 cells, four-phase counts/sums and grid hash. The fixed D65 transform
has three finite negative-blue cells: indices 119, 141 and 273, with blue values
-4.365410128164944, -3.2801891599251842 and -0.45283151275686606. Multiplying by
positive scale 1, 4 or 16 cannot remove their negative sign. The A transform has
no negative cells, but all three recorded runs choose the configured fallback.

The recovered format-0x11 reader floor max(1, s16(u32(numerator)/u32(D))-offset)
acts after unsigned tuple input, division and narrowing. It does not specify
clipping a negative continuous cross-camera transform before tuple construction.
No reviewed producer contract proves such a conversion. Do not transplant that
floor into the phone transform to make the trial pass.

A concrete implementation can retain 12 MP photographic WB behavior while
advancing independently evidenced rendering controls, such as the recovered
Low/Medium/High contrast path being handled by root. For WB, bind future fixtures
to actual source identities and retain explicit rejection/fallback provenance.
The new scalar fixture is suitable for a bounded offline experiment once its
specific input-basis question is selected; it is not a new live scene heuristic.
